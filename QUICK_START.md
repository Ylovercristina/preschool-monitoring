## Getting Started

### For Administrators
1. Log in with your admin account
2. Go to **Settings** → **Google Calendar Setup**
3. Follow the setup wizard to connect your Google Calendar
4. Events from your Google Calendar will now appear in the school system

### For Teachers
- Use the same interface as before
- Enjoy the cleaner, simpler design
- All your tools work exactly the same way
- Calendar now shows integrated events

### For Parents
- View school calendar with all events
- See both school-created events and Google Calendar events
- Cleaner, easier-to-read layout
- Better event details display

## Troubleshooting

### Calendar not showing events?
1. Go to admin setup page
2. Click "Connect to Google Calendar"
3. Verify configuration is correct
4. Refresh the page

### Colors look different?
- Clear your browser cache
- Hard refresh (Ctrl+Shift+R or Cmd+Shift+R)
- Try a different browser

### Something not working?
1. Check the GOOGLE_CALENDAR_SETUP.md file
2. Verify all setup steps are complete
3. Contact your administrator

## FAQ

**Q: Why did the colors change?**
A: The new design matches your "Play is School" logo for better brand consistency.

**Q: Do I need to do anything to use Google Calendar?**
A: Only admins need to set it up. Once configured, everyone can use it automatically.

**Q: Will my old data still be there?**
A: Yes! All your data is preserved. Only the appearance has changed.

**Q: Is my calendar data safe with Google?**
A: Yes, Google Calendar uses industry-standard security. Only your school's events sync.

**Q: Can I customize the colors?**
A: The colors are part of the official branding. Contact your administrator for questions.

**Q: What if I prefer the old design?**
A: The new design is what your school has decided to use. If you have concerns, speak with your administrator.
